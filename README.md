# ROLLSCAPE

> **$${\color{red}WARNING: \space ROLLSCAPE \space IS \space IN \space PRE-PRE \space ALPHA.}$$** Expect chaotic bugs, clunky gameplay, and crappy broken physics.

Welcome to your eternal prison.
You (the ball) have been brought down to ROLLSCAPE as punishment for *countless* war crimes against others using weaponry in previous ROLLER games.
You will be figHting  for survival in an EndLess geometric Plane. Choose from **4 distinct classes** to manipulate the arena, build momentum, and reach the finish after collecting all RED tessaracts. Use your heavy weapons arsenal to blow back the onslaught—they might fall for a moment, but they *always* respawn.

---

## $${\color{cyan}How \space to \space Survive}$$

*   **A / D** (or **Left / Right**) — Roll / Move
*   **SPACE** (or **Up Arrow**) — Jump
*   **MOUSE 1** — Fire Primary Weapon *(Bazooka, AK-47, Pistol, Katana)*
*   **E** — Activate Class Ability
*   **R** — Restart Run

---

## $${\color{cyan}How \space to \space use \space your \space class}$$

*   **KATANA** — Short-ranged; you can't fight with this normally. You can, however, $${\color{orange}PARRY}$$ enemies with this.
*   **PISTOLS** — Weak sidearms. Press **E** to immediately change direction and dash; $${\color{lightgreen}grants \space invincibility}$$ while dashing.
*   **TOMAHAWK** — Extremely light tomahawks. Throw some boring axes; ability throws multiple at once. *Provides no mobility.*
*   **BAZOOKA** — $${\color{orange}Hard-hitting \space missiles}$$ that break terrain and fling you far; be careful, $${\color{red}no \space ability.}$$

## $${\color{red}Threat \space Roster}$$

### Enemies
*   🟢 **Green Ball (4?)** — Bouncy anomalies that track and launch themselves directly at you.
*   📦 **Cuboid** — A cluster of 3 trailing cubes tracking your location. Their velocity scales the closer you get.
*   🔻 **Drill** — Charges and executes high-speed dashes. $${\color{red}Keep \space out \space of \space its \space horizontal \space path.}$$
*   🛸 **Drone** — Hovers persistently above your position before raining down targeted projectile volleys.
*   🃏 **Domino** — Launches its body near your position; $${\color{orange}watch \space out \space for \space its \space warning.}$$ Only the landing itself (5 frames) is deadly.
*   🚀 **Rocket** — A warning appears above you, then a missile drops down that column and explodes in a circle.
*   🍄 **Mold** — Dark green colony that slowly creeps over the terrain, growing 1 HP spore plants and 5 HP hopping rollers. Blow up the blocks it grows on to cut it back.

### Dimensional variants
Every enemy has a superbuffed **Dimensional** version (orange aura). It shows up as the 3rd copy of an enemy you pick, or as a rare card (10%): Cuboid (spits agar.io-style clones), Drone (rockets + slow-turning laser), Roller (re-times its delay, never under 0.6s), Drill (3-dash chains), Green Ball (landing shockwaves), Domino (3 slams), ASE (shot ring on teleport), Rocket (4-missile salvo), Mold (grows weaker mold versions of every basic enemy).

### One-time upgrades (gold screen)
*   **Matrix Tesseract** (20 gold) — flat movement speed, no momentum. Knockback still applies.
*   **Jet Boots** (30 gold) — hold jump in the air to fly up for 0.5s; refuels in 2s on the ground.
